Quick fixes for everyday tech trouble.

Simple steps you can try safely. If they do not solve the problem, send us a message.

Wi-Fi will not connect

  1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
  2. Restart your laptop.
  3. Forget the network, then reconnect using the correct details.

Laptop is very slow

  1. Restart your laptop.
  2. Close unused browser tabs and programs.
  3. Check that storage is not almost full.

Office says “View Only”

  1. Check you are signed in to the correct account.
  2. Save a copy of your work before trying changes.
  3. Contact us for help with your licensed setup.

Closed a browser tab

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + T.
  2. Repeat to restore more recently closed tabs.
